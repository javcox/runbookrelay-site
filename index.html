What we fix

The same operational leaks keep showing up in different industries.

The surface issue changes by business, but the underlying pattern is usually the same: slow response, weak follow-up, dropped context, avoidable admin drag, and too much work living in people's heads instead of a usable system.

Revenue leakage

Leads go cold because response speed is inconsistent.

Calls, forms, DMs, or consultation requests sit too long, and the business loses the moment when intent is highest.

Follow-up leakage

Good opportunities stall because ownership is too loose.

Teams tell themselves they followed up, but the work really lives in memory, scattered inboxes, or whoever happened to be available.

Execution drag

Manual handoffs create friction, delay, and dropped context.

Sales to intake, intake to service, dispatch to field, or manager to team - the same gaps keep reopening because the workflow is too informal.

Admin overload

Repetitive work quietly steals time every week.

Reports get rebuilt, tasks get re-entered, reminders get sent by hand, and the team keeps paying for a system that was never cleaned up.

What happens in the audit?

We identify the most expensive operational leak first. That might be slow response, weak follow-up, manual handoff friction, reporting drag, or repetitive admin work. If there is no clear path to value, we will say so.

Who is the best fit?

Small businesses and service businesses with real operational complexity: law firms, med spas, home services, and other teams where work leaks because the workflow is too manual or too inconsistent.

Is this an AI agency engagement?

No. Runbook Relay is an operator-led systems and automation consultancy. The point is better execution and revenue capture, not selling generic AI language.

Do you build giant custom systems?

Not by default. The work is intentionally narrow and practical. Clean up the leak, install the workflow, and leave the business with a clearer operating rhythm first.

How is the engagement framed?

The starting path is a booked audit and a fixed-scope implementation if the fit is clear. That keeps the work easier to approve, easier to run, and easier to measure.
