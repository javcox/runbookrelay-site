Working style

Sharp diagnosis. Narrow scope. Practical install.

Runbook Relay is built around one principle: if the leak is real, the solution should be usable, contained, and tied to an actual business outcome.

Why this exists

Most small businesses do not need more complexity. They need cleaner workflow discipline.

  • Leads go cold because nobody owns the next response clearly.
  • Manual follow-up depends on memory instead of structure.
  • Handoffs lose context between front desk, sales, staff, or field teams.
  • Admin work piles up because the same repetitive tasks stay manual.

Who it fits

Best for operators already feeling the cost of slow response or messy execution.

  • Law firms where inquiry speed changes who signs first.
  • Med spas where consult demand leaks between inquiry and booking.
  • Home services teams where missed calls and handoffs cost jobs.
  • Ops-heavy service businesses where SOP drift and admin drag keep compounding.
