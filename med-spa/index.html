The missed-lead problem

Med spas leak demand quietly when the first response is too slow.

Response delay

Consult requests sit too long before anyone follows up.

Interest is hot for a short window. Delay changes booking behavior quickly.

Follow-up gap

Staff misses the second or third touch.

Without a tighter system, a good lead can disappear after one missed step.

Channel sprawl

Calls, forms, DMs, and texts do not all live in the same rhythm.

That makes it easy to feel busy while still leaking appointment demand.

Reactivation

No-show and lost-consult follow-up is too inconsistent.

Good revenue often sits in old conversations nobody is working methodically.

Booking recovery system

The first job is getting demand contacted and booked faster.

  • Faster follow-up for new consult requests
  • Cleaner staff ownership after form, DM, call, or missed contact
  • Less revenue leakage between "interested" and "booked"

No-show and reactivation

The second job is recovering demand that should not have been lost.

  • No-show follow-up logic that does not depend on memory
  • Reactivation workflow for older consult interest
  • Cleaner handoff between front desk, coordinator, and booking owner

FAQ

What kind of med spa fits best?

Clinics already generating consult demand that still feel loose or inconsistent in response, booking, and follow-up.

Is this an ad management service?

No. The point is to convert more of the demand already reaching the business instead of leaking it through weak follow-up.

Does this replace my staff?

No. It gives staff a cleaner workflow to run so good leads do not depend on memory and improvisation.
