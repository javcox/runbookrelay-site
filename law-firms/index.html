Speed-to-lead

The first fix is usually response speed, not more traffic.

If the firm is already paying for ads, referrals, or local visibility, the highest-leverage improvement is often tighter intake response and routing.

  • Reduce lag between inquiry and first firm response
  • Clarify who owns each step after call, form, or missed contact
  • Remove avoidable handoff delay between intake and decision-making

Follow-up system

The second fix is keeping the lead from disappearing.

  • Cleaner follow-up rhythm after the first response
  • Missed call and missed-contact recovery logic
  • Clear consult next-step handling so good matters do not stall in limbo
